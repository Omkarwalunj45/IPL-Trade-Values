# Temporal backtest

Everything here is frozen before the trade it judges. WAR for season Y is built only
from Y-3..Y-1, so `war_2024.csv` has never seen a ball bowled in 2024.

## Run

    python backtest/validate_projection.py     # rebuilds war_20XX.csv, scores players
    python backtest/cash_trade_roi.py          # cash paid against WAR delivered

## What each one tests

`validate_projection.py` asks whether pWAR, built only on prior seasons, predicts what
a player was actually worth afterwards. Rank correlation 0.628 on nine trades. It also
writes the four vintage tables into `Datasets/`.

It does **not** ask whether the trade was good for the club. That needs squads,
salaries and purses as they stood on the day, run through the XII optimiser. Auction
prices only start at 2023 and no purse history exists, so club-level scoring is not
possible yet. Do not report these columns as a verdict on a trade.

`cash_trade_roi.py` is the part that does stand on its own: cash paid against WAR
delivered, which needs no squad or purse data. Headline results:

| Year | Player | Fee | WAR before | WAR after | Rs cr / expected WAR | / delivered WAR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024 | Cameron Green | 17.50 | 0.77 | 0.87 | 22.8 | 20.0 |
| 2024 | Hardik Pandya | 15.00 | 0.71 | 0.60 | 21.1 | 24.8 |
| 2023 | Shardul Thakur | 10.75 | 0.25 | 0.16 | 43.6 | 67.8 |
| 2023 | Lockie Ferguson | 10.00 | 0.53 | -0.34 | 18.8 | negative |
| 2021 | Harshal Patel | 0.20 | -0.35 | +2.29 | - | 0.1 |

Shardul was the worst value in the set before a ball was bowled, at twice the cost per
win of either 2024 blockbuster. Harshal is the counterexample and belongs in any
write-up: the model had him below replacement and he delivered +2.29.

## Seasons covered

2023 and 2024 trades are scorable. 2021 and 2022 are not: the trades file records none
for those years, and auction prices do not go back before 2023. 2019 and 2020 cash
trades sit in `ipl_cash_trades.csv` but are excluded, because WAR for 2020 would need
2017 and the ball-by-ball starts at 2018.

## Caveat on the fees

Hardik's Rs 15cr was reported as a fee plus an undisclosed transfer component, and
several deals were partly salary absorption rather than cash to the selling club.
Treat cost-per-WAR as an order-of-magnitude comparison, not a precise price.
